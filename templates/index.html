FEATURES & CAPABILITIES

What Agent2Hire does for your organisation.

1
JD-Based Candidate Matching

You provide the job description. Agent2Hire reads and structures your requirements and uses them as the benchmark for every candidate evaluation — no manual criteria-setting needed.

2
Automated Resume Parsing

Resumes are parsed at scale and matched against your JD criteria — skills, experience, and role fit — eliminating hours of manual CV review from your hiring team.

3
AI Shortlisting

The best-fit candidates from the parsed pool are automatically filtered and ranked, so your team only sees candidates who meet the bar — not everyone who applied.

4
Voice Agent Screening

Shortlisted candidates go through an AI-powered voice screening. The agent asks role-relevant questions, evaluates responses, and scores candidates without a recruiter on the call.

5
Objective Scoring & Ranking

Every candidate receives a transparent score based on consistent criteria. No fatigue, no unconscious bias — just a clean ranked list your team can act on.

6
Final Shortlist Delivery

A ranked, voice-screened shortlist is delivered to your hiring team — interview-ready, with scores and screening summaries attached per candidate.
